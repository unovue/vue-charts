SVG has no `z-index`: an element painted later covers an element painted earlier. A chart
paints its SVG content in three tiers, bottom to top.

1. **Template content and the tooltip cursor.** The grid, axes, reference elements and your
   own [custom overlays](/components/custom-overlays) paint in template order. The tooltip
   cursor paints above them.
2. **Series.** `Area`, `Bar`, `Line`, `Scatter` and `Radar` paint in template order. A series
   declared later covers a series declared earlier, whatever its type. `Pie`, `RadialBar` and
   `Funnel` paint with the template content in tier 1, also in template order.
3. **Labels.** `LabelList` and series labels paint above all series, so a series never
   hides a label.

The tooltip and the legend are HTML elements on top of the SVG.

## Change the order

Reorder the series in the template. Here the `Line` is declared before the `Area`, so the
opaque `Area` covers it:

```vue
<ComposedChart :data="data">
  <Line data-key="signups" />
  <Area data-key="visits" :fill-opacity="0.9" />
</ComposedChart>
```

::chart-demo{name="Template order" description="The Line is declared first, so the Area paints over it." src="composed-charts/template-order-chart"}
::

Declare the `Area` first to put the `Line` on top. The order is the same on the server, after
hydration, with reduced motion, and after data updates.
