Three common dashboard pieces need no new component: they are a few props on charts you already have.

## Donut with a center value

An inner radius turns a pie into a donut; a `<Label position="center">` inside the `<Pie>` puts the total in the hole. `padding-angle` and `corner-radius` separate the slices.

::chart-demo{name="Donut" description="Browser share with the total in the center." src="recipe-charts/donut-center-value-chart"}
::

## Gauge

A gauge is a radial bar with one value. `start-angle` and `end-angle` open the ring at the bottom, a number `PolarAngleAxis` with `domain` `[0, 100]` makes the value a percentage of the full arc, and `background` draws the empty track.

::chart-demo{name="Gauge" description="Progress toward a monthly goal." src="recipe-charts/gauge-chart"}
::

## Conversion funnel

A horizontal bar chart shows each step against the first, the bar `background` shows what was lost, and a `LabelList` on the right shows the conversion from the step before.

::chart-demo{name="Conversion Funnel" description="Users through four steps with step-to-step conversion." src="recipe-charts/conversion-funnel-chart"}
::
