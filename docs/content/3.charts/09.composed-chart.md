Composed charts let you mix multiple chart types — Area, Bar, Line, and Scatter — in a single container.

## Usage

Use `<ComposedChart>` instead of a specific chart container. Place any combination of `<Area>`, `<Bar>`, `<Line>`, or `<Scatter>` inside:

```vue
<ComposedChart :data="data">
  <CartesianGrid stroke-dasharray="3 3" />
  <XAxis data-key="name" />
  <YAxis />
  <Tooltip />
  <Area data-key="amt" fill="#f97316" stroke="#f97316" :fill-opacity="0.3" />
  <Bar data-key="pv" fill="#14b8a6" :bar-size="20" />
  <Line data-key="uv" stroke="#f59e0b" />
</ComposedChart>
```

Series paint in template order: a series declared later covers the ones declared before it. See the [Z-Order and Layers](/guides/z-order-and-layers) guide for details.

## ComposedChart props

`<ComposedChart>` takes the [chart container props](/charts/chart-containers), including bar
sizing. Its tooltip always follows the category axis.
